About Me

My AI Journey

From discovering the power of an LSTM neural network, more than 7 years ago, I ended up in the states pursuing a career in AI. I am currently a hands-on AI Engineering Manager who enjoys the trip from and early PoC or research prototype to a production system. During my career at SvcLnk, my work has combined applied ML, GenAI, and heavy software engineering. I currently enjoy a lot working with my team and watching them grow and suceed

Research foundation

I have experience researching machine-learning fairness, privacy, internet measurement, and graph analysis. That work led to presentations at ICML, Euro S&P, and SECRYPT, including a Best Presentation award at the Euro S&P workshop. One of the biggest lessons from this period is to never make assumptions. We always need the data to back our decisions, and every decision we take has to be evidence-based and with strong foundations.

Outside the work

I enjoy music, soccer, and exploring a city on foot. I'm a big traveler, even though I always think I should travel more!
